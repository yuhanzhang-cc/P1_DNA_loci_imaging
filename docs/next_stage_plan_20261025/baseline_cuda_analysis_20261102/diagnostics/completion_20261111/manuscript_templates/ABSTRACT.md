# Offset-supervision alignment for DNA-locus detection in fluorescence microscopy

**Working title; prospective research manuscript, not a completed study.**

Reliable localization of fluorescent DNA loci requires both spot detection and subpixel coordinate estimation. We investigate whether the relationship between a predicted heatmap peak and the pixels receiving offset supervision limits localization in a small, curated microscopy benchmark. We assembled 220 image–annotation pairs and fixed a development partition of 176 training and 22 validation images; the remaining historical partition is excluded from new claims of independent validation. We compare direct grid-based and dense heatmap references with deterministic scale-space methods and a locally adapted external detector. A parameter-identical signed-offset control and a neighborhood-supervision model isolate two sequential changes while preserving the backbone and development selection budget.
